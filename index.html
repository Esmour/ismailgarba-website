A personal introduction to my research and work.

I am an agricultural systems scientist and Research Scientist at CSIRO, working across crop modelling, farming systems analysis and data-driven approaches to agricultural research.

My work uses simulation models and quantitative methods to investigate how crops and farming systems respond to management, climate and environmental conditions. I am particularly interested in translating complex model outputs into evidence that can inform research, decision-making and sustainable agricultural practice.

Across research projects, I work with tools and workflows that connect process-based crop modelling, large datasets, statistical analysis and interactive decision-support applications.
