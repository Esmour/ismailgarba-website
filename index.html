A personal introduction to my research and work.

I integrate agronomy, crop modelling and data analytics to understand how farming systems perform across environments.

My research examines genotype × environment × management interactions and how management, climate and environmental conditions shape crop productivity, resilience and resource-use efficiency. I develop analytical frameworks that combine process-based crop models such as APSIM with experimental data, climate information and digital agriculture datasets.

My work brings together biological science, data engineering and quantitative analysis to generate system-level insights for agricultural research and decision-making. My areas of expertise include farming systems modelling, environmental characterisation (envirotyping), data integration and large-scale analysis of agricultural datasets.

04 / META-ANALYSIS

COVER CROPS · WATER-LIMITED SYSTEMS

Rainfall requirements for cover-cropping benefits

A meta-analysis examining how cover crops affect subsequent cash-crop yield, soil water and soil mineral nitrogen across climates, soils and management conditions. The GRDC summary reports an estimated 700 mm annual rainfall break-even threshold, while emphasising substantial context dependence.
