**Dr Ismail Ibrahim Garba** is an agricultural systems scientist with experience in crop and farming systems modelling, agronomy, experimental research and agricultural data analytics. My work combines APSIM simulations, field evidence, environmental datasets and statistical analysis to investigate G × E × M interactions, nitrogen management, crop productivity and farming-system performance.

[CSIRO profile](https://people.csiro.au/g/i/ismail-garba) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pylRZsQAAAAJ&hl=en) · [ORCID 0000-0002-0009-4866](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0009-4866)

## Education

- **PhD, Agronomy and Plant Science**, The University of Queensland, 2020–2023. Thesis: *Cover cropping in drylands for improved agronomic and environmental outcomes.*
- **MSc, Agronomy (Crops and Cropping Systems in Drylands)**, Bayero University Kano, 2016–2018. CGPA: 5.00/5.00.
- **B Agric (Hons)**, Bayero University Kano, 2010–2015. First Class; CGPA: 4.81/5.00.

## Professional experience

- **Research Scientist, CSIRO Agriculture and Food**, January 2024–present. Digital Innovations Program, Integration and Analysis. Crop modelling and analysis across RiskWi$e, profitable yield frontiers/hyper-yielding crops, BNI modelling and Northern Farming Systems.
- **Postdoctoral Research Fellow, The University of Queensland (INVITA)**, March 2023–January 2024. Genotype calibration and multi-criteria optimisation; automated R/Python pipelines for soil and climate input generation; integration of APSIM, remote sensing and field trials.
- **Technical Officer – Agronomy, The University of Queensland**, March 2021–January 2023. Supported practical teaching, field trials, data summaries, reports, analysis, facilities and safety processes.
- **Casual Academic, The University of Queensland**, August 2020–December 2022. Teaching, marking and course administration across undergraduate and postgraduate agronomy courses.
- **Research Fellow II, Centre for Dryland Agriculture, Bayero University Kano**, November 2018–December 2019. Teaching, postgraduate research support, and field and laboratory experiments.
- **Research Assistant, TAMASA, Bayero University Kano**, June 2015–August 2018. CERES-Maize parameterisation; soil, crop and weather data analysis; decision-support tool testing; nutrient omission and farmer-participatory trials.
- **Research Associate, eHealth Systems Africa**, February–June 2017. Nutrition and food-security surveys, desk reviews and data analysis.

## Research skills and methods

- **Crop modelling:** APSIM Classic, APSIM Next Generation and DSSAT, including CERES-Maize and CROPGRO.
- **Programming and data science:** R, Python, LaTeX and Excel VBA; data processing, reproducible workflows and visualisation.
- **Agronomic analysis:** crop nutrition, nutrient diagnosis, QUEFTS, Compositional Nutrient Diagnosis (CND), DRIS and crop-response analysis.
- **Quantitative methods:** statistical analysis, multi-criteria optimisation, simulation experiments, environmental characterisation and G × E × M analysis.
- **Computing:** parallel and cloud/HPC workflows, including Google Colab, Bunya, Petrichor and Singularity containers; Windows and Linux Ubuntu.
