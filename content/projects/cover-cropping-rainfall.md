My doctoral research at The University of Queensland investigated cover cropping in drylands, including agronomic and environmental outcomes of cover crop mixtures. The work developed and validated a crop-mixture experimental methodology, integrating field experimentation, remote sensing, statistical analysis and APSIM modelling. It examined the implications of using cover crops as fallow replacements in the subtropical Northern Grain Region of Australia.

A related GRDC GroundCover article, **“Rainfall requirements for cover cropping benefits”**, discusses how rainfall environment influences the potential benefits and trade-offs associated with cover cropping. The implications of cover cropping depend on environmental and management context; consult the article for its evidence and interpretation.
