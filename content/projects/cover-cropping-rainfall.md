The GRDC GroundCover feature summarises research on how cover crops influence subsequent cash-crop yield, soil water and soil mineral nitrogen across different rainfall environments. The article discusses an estimated annual rainfall break-even threshold of approximately 700 mm and emphasises that outcomes depend on environmental and management context.
