This research uses crop modelling and environmental characterisation to understand crop performance across locations, seasons, genotypes and management conditions. It brings together process-based simulations, field-trial results, climate and soil data and other environmental information to investigate attainable crop productivity and the drivers of variation.

A wheat study develops an envirotyping framework for genotype × management strategies to capture yield frontiers under a variable climate. A separate sorghum study integrates yield-gap analysis to examine genotype × environment × management interactions in Australian broadacre sorghum systems.
