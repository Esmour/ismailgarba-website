The National Risk Management Initiative (RiskWi$e; GRDC project CSP2303) is a collaborative research programme focused on understanding and managing risk in Australian farming systems. My documented role is **team member**, with my CSIRO CV describing my project contribution as crop modeller and analyst.

My work contributes crop-modelling and analytical perspectives to the assessment of production risk and management options. I have also individually developed digital tools, including RiskWi$e-N and the RiskWi$e Nitrogen Decision Dashboard, which are listed in [Software and digital tools](/software/).
