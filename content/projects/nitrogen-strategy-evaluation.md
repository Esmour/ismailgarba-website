This work develops data workflows and interactive visualisation concepts to evaluate nitrogen management strategies across simulated seasons and environments.

The analytical framework considers yield, economic returns, nitrogen supply and fertiliser dependency, nitrogen balances, environmental losses and the economic penalty associated with a strategy relative to the best-performing alternative within a simulation year.
