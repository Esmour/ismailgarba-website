This work uses crop simulation and analytical workflows to evaluate alternative nitrogen management strategies across seasons, environments and cropping systems. A particular focus is **strategic nitrogen banking**: considering nitrogen fertiliser decisions across crop sequences and assessing how nitrogen investment and carry-over influence productivity, profitability and environmental outcomes.

The analyses compare strategies using crop yield, income, costs and gross margins, nitrogen supply and fertiliser dependency, nitrogen balances and potential losses. They also examine how consistently a strategy performs relative to the best-performing alternative within a simulated season, providing a way to quantify the economic penalty or “shadow cost” associated with a decision.
