The GRDC project CSP2404-020RTX, *Hyper yielding and profitable crops*, investigates opportunities to improve crop performance and profitability. My documented role is **team member**. My CSIRO CV describes my contribution in the related *Exploring Profitable Yield Frontiers for Hyper Yielding Crops* work as farming-systems integration and analysis at scale.

Related research includes environmental characterisation and genotype × management analysis for wheat yield frontiers, and the integration of yield-gap analysis to understand G × E × M interactions in Australian sorghum systems. See [Yield frontiers, envirotyping and crop productivity](/project/global-yield-potential/).
