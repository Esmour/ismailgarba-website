This work develops an APSIM-based modelling framework to explore the plausible impacts of biological nitrification inhibition (BNI) in cropping systems. The framework examines crop productivity, nitrogen dynamics and potential environmental implications across contrasting production environments.

The cropping systems considered include sorghum in northern Australia, wheat in Western Australia and canola in South Australia. The modelling framework is intended to support scenario analysis; model limitations and the distinction between simulated responses and field validation must be made explicit when describing results.
