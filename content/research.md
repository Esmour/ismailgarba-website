My research applies agronomic knowledge, process-based crop modelling, experimentation and data analytics to understand crop performance and improve decisions across agricultural systems. I work across scales, from crop and soil processes to paddock and farming-system questions, combining simulations with experimental, environmental and digital datasets.

## Research themes

### Crop and farming systems modelling

Using APSIM Classic and APSIM Next Generation, alongside other crop-modelling approaches, to investigate crop performance, management strategies and farming-system behaviour across contrasting soils, climates and production environments. This includes large-scale simulation experiments, model parameterisation and calibration, scenario analysis, and reproducible workflows for building and analysing simulations.

### Genotype × environment × management interactions and envirotyping

Characterising environmental variation and management effects to understand why genotypes and crop systems perform differently across sites and seasons. This includes integrating crop models, field-trial information, soil and climate datasets and remote sensing to identify environmental drivers, interpret multi-environment evidence and investigate attainable yield frontiers.

### Nitrogen management and nitrogen-use efficiency

Evaluating nitrogen supply, fertiliser strategies, nitrogen banking and nitrogen dynamics in cropping systems. This work considers productivity and profitability alongside nitrogen balances and environmental losses, including nitrate leaching and gaseous emissions. An important objective is to understand the trade-offs and risks associated with alternative nitrogen strategies under seasonal variability.

### Biological nitrification inhibition

Using a process-based modelling framework to explore the plausible impacts of biological nitrification inhibition on nitrogen transformations, crop productivity and environmental outcomes. The modelling work spans contrasting Australian cropping systems and is intended for scenario analysis. Simulated outcomes should be interpreted in light of the framework's assumptions and validation limitations; they are not a substitute for field validation.

### Cover cropping and dryland production

Investigating how cover crop mixtures and dryland environments influence subsequent crop performance and resource dynamics. My doctoral research examined agronomic and environmental outcomes of cover cropping in drylands, integrating field experimentation, remote sensing, statistics and APSIM.

### Agricultural data science and decision support

Developing reproducible analytical workflows and **individually developed digital tools** for integrating simulation outputs, experiments, environmental data and decision-relevant indicators. I use R, Python and data technologies to process large datasets, analyse uncertainty and variability, and communicate results through interactive applications and visualisations.

## Methods and technologies

APSIM Classic and APSIM Next Generation; DSSAT; R; Python; SQL; statistical analysis; multi-criteria optimisation; crop-model calibration; experimental design; environmental characterisation; and integration of experimental, soil, climate, remote-sensing and model-generated data. Computing environments used include Google Colab, Bunya, Petrichor, Singularity containers, Linux and Windows.
