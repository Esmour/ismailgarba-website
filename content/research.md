My research uses quantitative and process-based approaches to address questions in agricultural productivity, resource-use efficiency and farming-system resilience.

## Research themes

### Crop and farming systems modelling

Applying APSIM and related analytical approaches to investigate crop performance, management options and system behaviour across contrasting soils, climates and production environments.

### Nitrogen management and biological nitrification inhibition

Investigating nitrogen supply, fertiliser strategies, nitrogen cycling and the plausible impacts of biological nitrification inhibition on crop productivity and environmental outcomes.

### Genotype × environment × management interactions

Characterising environmental variation and management effects to understand differences in crop performance and support interpretation of multi-environment evidence.

### Agricultural data science and decision support

Developing reproducible workflows for simulation experiments, data integration, statistical analysis, visualisation and decision-support tools.

### Climate risk and adaptation

Using long-term simulations and environmental data to examine climate variability, crop productivity and management trade-offs.
