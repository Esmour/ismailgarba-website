## Software and digital tools

I have **individually developed all the software applications and digital tools listed in this section**. These tools translate crop modelling, field research and data analytics into interactive resources for exploring crop choice, nitrogen management, yield potential, profitability and production risk.

### RiskWi$e-N

A digital application to help users make nitrogen management decisions more often under uncertainty.

[Open RiskWi$e-N](https://shiny.csiro.au/Riskwise-n/) · [CSIRO software record, version 2](https://doi.org/10.25919/f9b1-ed45)

### cropReckoner

An interactive tool for weighing up which crop is best suited to a particular situation. It evaluates relative risks for different crop options using information such as sowing conditions, soil constraints, disease inoculum, weed populations and crop history. It also explores implications for future crop choices and the suitability of the next crop in sequence.

[Open cropReckoner](https://01a1ec16-a008-ddf0-87e0-46fb2685477d.share.connect.posit.cloud/)

### RiskWi$e Nitrogen Decision Dashboard

A dashboard for evaluating risk–reward trade-offs in nitrogen decisions across productivity, profitability and environmental outcomes.

[Open the RiskWi$e Nitrogen Decision Dashboard](https://farmsys.shinyapps.io/riskwise_nitrogen_decision_dashboard_v1/)

### Enterprise Decision Reckoner

An analytical decision-support application integrating more than ten years of field experiments, APSIM simulations and analytics to guide cropping-intensity and diversity decisions.

[Open Enterprise Decision Reckoner](https://01a1ed2a-adea-f6b3-abf7-e551e1c18f64.share.connect.posit.cloud/)

### PYw Rainbow and N Budgeting Tools

Tools using APSIM-simulated yield outcomes to explore crop yield probabilities across paddocks, crops, sowing dates and starting soil-water levels. The tools provide probability-based forecasts of water-limited yield potential for a given starting soil-water condition and sowing date across locations, supporting planting decisions.

[Open PYw Rainbow and N Budgeting Tools](https://esmour-farmingsystems-rainbow-and-n-budgeting.share.connect.posit.cloud/)

### Nitrogen Decision Explorer

A scenario-based application providing insights to support nitrogen decision-making under seasonal and production variability.

[Open Nitrogen Decision Explorer](https://esmour-riskwise-n-bank-explorer-v3.share.connect.posit.cloud/)

## Analytical methods and technologies

Research workflows use APSIM Classic and APSIM Next Generation, DSSAT, R, Python and SQL. Data and visualisation technologies include data.table, Arrow, DuckDB, Shiny, Plotly and Leaflet. Methods include simulation design, crop-model calibration, statistical analysis, multi-criteria optimisation, environmental characterisation and integration of experimental, soil, climate, remote-sensing and model-generated data.
