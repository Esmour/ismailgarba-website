I am **Dr Ismail Ibrahim Garba**, an agricultural systems scientist working at the intersection of agronomy, crop modelling and data analytics. My work brings together process-based crop models, experimental evidence, climate information and large-scale agricultural datasets to understand how farming systems perform across environments.

My research interests include genotype × environment × management interactions, farming systems analysis, nitrogen dynamics and management, biological nitrification inhibition, environmental characterisation (envirotyping), and reproducible agricultural data workflows.

I work as a Research Scientist with CSIRO. My academic qualifications include a PhD in Agronomy and Crop Science from The University of Queensland (2023), an MSc in Agronomy from Bayero University Kano (2018), and a Bachelor of Agriculture (Honours) from Bayero University Kano (2015).
