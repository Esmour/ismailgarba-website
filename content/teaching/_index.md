My teaching experience at The University of Queensland includes undergraduate and postgraduate agronomy and agricultural science courses. As a Casual Academic (August 2020–December 2022), I prepared and delivered lectures, tutorials, computer-based and field practicals, coordinated assessment marking and supported course administration across **AGRC7005, AGRC3040, AGRC3002, AGRC2040 and AGRC1024**.

I also worked as a Technical Officer – Agronomy (March 2021–January 2023), supporting practical teaching, field trials, data summaries, reports and teaching facilities.
