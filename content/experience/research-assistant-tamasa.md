**June 2015–August 2018** · Taking Maize Agronomy to Scale in Africa (TAMASA) project, Bayero University Kano.

- Parameterised CERES-Maize and used weather, soil and other datasets for scenario analysis.
- Collated and analysed spatial and temporal soil, crop and weather data using ODK, CSPRO and COSMO to support yield-gap and soil-constraint analysis and agronomy databases.
- Supported development and testing of decision-support systems and APIs, including the National Maize Variety Selector and Nutrient Expert tools, with maize-sector stakeholders in Nigeria.
- Designed, implemented and managed on-station and on-farm nutrient omission trials and farmer-participatory trials.
