**2020–2023** · The University of Queensland, Australia.

**Thesis:** *Cover cropping in drylands for improved agronomic and environmental outcomes.*

The doctoral research developed and validated a crop-mixture experimental methodology integrating field experimentation, remote sensing, statistical analysis and APSIM modelling. The work examined the agronomic and environmental implications of using cover crops as fallow replacements in the subtropical Northern Grain Region of Australia.
