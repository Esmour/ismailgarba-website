**January 2024–present** · Digital Innovations Program, Integration and Analysis Team.

My research at CSIRO applies agronomy, crop modelling and data analytics to questions in agricultural productivity, resource-use efficiency, profitability and environmental performance. I contribute modelling and analytical work to national and cross-disciplinary research programmes.

Selected programme contributions include:

- **National Risk Management Initiative (RiskWi$e):** crop modelling and analysis of production risk and management decisions. Appendix A records my project role as team member; my CV describes my work as crop modeller and analyst.
- **Profitable Yield Frontiers / Hyper Yielding and Profitable Crops:** farming-systems integration and analysis at scale, including crop performance and environmental variation.
- **Biological nitrification inhibition (BNI):** modelling plausible production and environmental impacts in Australian farming systems. My recorded project roles include team member on GRDC project CSP2406-021RTX and Output III co-lead on Proc-9177320, Improving systems nitrogen-use efficiency and reducing losses through BNI.
- **Northern Farming Systems Phase 2:** research integration and analysis to support profitable farming-system outcomes. My recorded role is team member.
- **Nitrogen management:** simulation and analysis of nitrogen supply, fertiliser strategies, nitrogen banking, productivity, profitability and environmental outcomes.

I also **individually develop** digital applications and analytical tools that translate modelling and research outputs into interactive decision-support resources. See [Software and digital tools](/software/).
